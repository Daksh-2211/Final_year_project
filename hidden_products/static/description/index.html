Website Hidden Product

Sell products directly by hiding them from the Odoo website!

Website Hidden Product allows you to hide products from the Odoo website. Moreover, the hidden products are available for direct selling using the product link or URL. You can configure hidden products from the setting menu and website setting menu.

Why Website Hidden Product?

Selling some products on customers priority and demand makes your website trustworthy. Also, it gives them a sense of security that whenever they need a product, it will be available on your Odoo website.


Hidden products are available for sale by direct link so your priority and regular customer (who buy the product regularly) can purchase the product you want to hide from the shop page.


Hide Products for Direct Sale in Odoo!

Few eCommerce vendors want to provide the request-based product view. With this Odoo app, the admin can hide products for direct sale on the Odoo website. Using this module, you can hide products under optional, service, and virtual categories from the Odoo website.


Vendors aim to build products that best serve customers and provide them with a good user experience. Hence, the Odoo user can only view these products on the cart page instead of the sale page.
