Seamless Seller-wise Checkout

Odoo Marketplace Seller Wise Checkout module enhances the shopping experience by allowing customers to purchase from multiple sellers in a single session without losing any cart items. This feature also benefits marketplace owners by encouraging customers to return for future purchases, as it eliminates the frustration of re-adding items to the cart. Additionally, it offers greater flexibility in managing deliveries and payments, as customers can handle transactions for each seller independently, improving convenience and efficiency.

Simplified Order Management

Odoo Marketplace Seller Wise Checkout module enhances backend efficiency by automatically splitting orders based on the seller. Admins can view detailed order information for each seller, making it easier to oversee the entire fulfillment process. Additionally, this streamlined approach reduces confusion and operational complexity, leading to faster and more accurate order processing. Sellers can also manage their own orders, providing full control over their products and ensuring a smooth, organized marketplace experience for both parties.

Flexible Cart Experience

Odoo Marketplace Seller Wise Checkout module allows customers to retain items from different sellers in their cart for future purchases. Even after completing a checkout for one seller, products from other sellers remain saved in the cart. This feature allows customers to continue shopping without losing their selections, providing the flexibility to purchase items conveniently. It also encourages higher purchase volumes, as customers can return to their saved items later.

Detailed Features List

Seller-wise Checkout

  • Customers can place orders and pay separately for products from different sellers.
  • Each seller's order is handled individually, reducing checkout confusion.
  • It simplifies payment process by breaking down the cart into seller-specific orders.

Separate Order Management

  • Admins can view and manage orders for each seller separately.
  • Improved backend order tracking and processing, ensuring smooth fulfilment.
  • Sellers can also track their orders and manage order lines individually.

Flexible Cart Retention

  • Customers can retain products from other sellers even after completing an order.
  • The cart remains populated with items for future purchases, encouraging repeat sales.
  • A Pop-up cart shows the remaining items and allows quick access to other orders.

Seller Profile Access

  • Customers can visit the seller's profile directly from the cart.
  • Enhances the visibility of sellers, boosting trust and potential future sales.
  • Simplifies navigation between seller profiles and product listings.
